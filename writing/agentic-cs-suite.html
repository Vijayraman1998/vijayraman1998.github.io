Illustrative PRD

Agentic AI Customer-Service Suite

Author: Vijay Raman · Illustrative · Type: Platform

An illustrative PRD in my own words, describing the kind of system I worked on. It uses no internal documents, code, client names or data.

01Problem

Enterprise clients in lending, automotive and sales handle large volumes of routine customer conversations. They need AI agents that resolve those conversations end to end, hand off cleanly when a human is needed, and stay accurate and cost-bounded enough to trust in production.

02Goals & non-goals

Goals

  • Resolve routine conversations with no human transfer
  • Keep answers faithful to the client's own data, at a bounded cost per conversation
  • Hand off to a human agent on explicit escalation triggers

Non-goals

  • Replacing human agents on complex or edge cases
  • Channel transport (covered by the WhatsApp queue PRD)

03Users

  • End customers want fast, correct answers.
  • Client product, operations and compliance teams want automation plus control over scope and escalation rules.
  • Internal engineering and ops need predictable cost and latency.

04Requirements, prioritized

PriorityRequirement
P0Defined agent scope per client, with typed tool calling on Azure OpenAI
P0Escalation flow to a human agent on explicit escalation triggers
P0Output guardrails, and structured JSON output validated before any CRM write
P1Self-serve configuration so client teams launch agents, prompts and tools without engineering
P1Evaluation on labeled sets from real client traffic, scoring retrieval and generation separately

05Key trade-offs

  • Escalation before autonomy. Explicit escalation triggers bound what the agent attempts alone, trading a little capability for predictable cost and caller experience. Containment only counts when the caller did not need a human.
  • Fix retrieval before buying a bigger model. Scoring retrieval and generation separately showed that most failures were in retrieval, which is cheaper to fix than a model upgrade.
  • Guardrails as a product surface. Validated output and clear hand-off rules are what make clients willing to put an agent in front of real customers.

06Success metrics

  • 70%+ automated containment, with explicit escalation triggers
  • 50–80% lower cost per conversation than a human agent, modeled per client on that client's own call mix
  • Average handle time 10–30% shorter on most use cases, modeled per client on that client's own call mix
  • Answer faithfulness scored on labeled sets from real client traffic (up 35% after the retrieval fix)

07Outcome

Shipped across 15+ production deployments for 5 enterprise clients.
