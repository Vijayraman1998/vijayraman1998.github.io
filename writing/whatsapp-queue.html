Illustrative PRD

Multi-Tenant WhatsApp Queue

Author: Vijay Raman · Illustrative · Type: Scale-up

An illustrative PRD in my own words, describing the kind of system I worked on. It uses no internal documents, code, client names or data.

01Problem

Enterprise clients run customer messaging on WhatsApp. When many tenant accounts with different message priorities share one pipeline, the queue has to keep tenants isolated, respect priority and stay inside the platform's API limits as traffic grows.

02Goals & non-goals

Goals

  • Serve 200+ tenant accounts concurrently with strict isolation
  • Zero WhatsApp API throttling
  • Hold the delivery SLA under 3x peak traffic

Non-goals

  • Conversation logic (covered by the agentic suite, a separate PRD)
  • Tenant billing and onboarding UI

03Users & use cases

  • Enterprise tenant ops teams send time-sensitive (P1), standard (P2) and bulk (P3) messages, and expect priority to be respected.
  • Platform and SRE need predictable throughput and no cascading failures.

04Requirements, prioritized

PriorityRequirement
P0Per-tenant isolation so no tenant can exhaust another's quota
P0Multi-priority routing: P1 > P2 > P3
P0Respect WhatsApp API rate limits, with zero throttling
P1Concurrency to absorb 3x peak (50-thread design)

05Key trade-offs

  • Priority model over pure FIFO. P1/P2/P3 routing so time-sensitive messages never queue behind bulk campaigns.
  • Bounded concurrency. A 50-thread design with tenant isolation, sized to absorb 3x peak traffic at full SLA.

06Success metrics

  • 0 throttling incidents over 60+ days (primary)
  • Full SLA held under 3x peak traffic
  • 200+ tenant accounts on the queue

07Outcome

Shipped and stable: zero throttling over 60+ days, and 3x peak absorbed at full SLA.
